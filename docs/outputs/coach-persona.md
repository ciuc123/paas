# Coach Persona: Early Adopter Profile

**Status:** Example output — not yet validated with real coach interviews.

---

## Primary Persona: The Solo Life Coach

**Name archetype:** Independent practitioner, 2–8 years experience, primarily online.

**Audience:** Individual clients seeking personal transformation (career change, confidence, relationships).

**Current tool stack:** Notion for content, Calendly for bookings, Google Drive for resources, email for delivery.

**Key pain points:**
- Spends significant time manually sharing PDFs, links, and instructions with each new client.
- No single place for clients to access materials — scattered across Drive, email threads, and Notion links.
- Wants to look professional but lacks technical capacity to build a proper client portal.
- Afraid of over-committing to expensive software before knowing if clients will use it.

**Willingness to pay:** $29–$59/month if the tool saves more than 2 hours/week of admin overhead.

**Decision trigger:** Seeing a working demo of a branded mini-portal built in under 15 minutes.

---

## Secondary Persona: The Online Fitness Coach

**Audience:** Remote clients on structured 8–12 week programs.

**Pain point:** Delivering program PDFs and videos without a proper client-facing hub.

**Differentiator need:** Wants clients to feel they are inside a real product, not just receiving a Google Drive link.

---

## Early Adopter Signal Criteria

- Already selling coaching online (not just considering it).
- Has at least 5 active clients.
- Spends time each week on manual content delivery.
- Has tried and rejected overly complex no-code tools.
